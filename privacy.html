Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data MindForge Ukraine LLC processes when you visit mindforgeukraine.com or contact us, why, and what rights you have.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is MindForge Ukraine LLC, company code 46090205, registered at 29 Yevhena Konovaltsia St., Kyiv 01133, Ukraine. Contact for privacy matters: projects@mindforgeukraine.com.

2. What we collect

  • Project requests sent through the website form or by email: your name, your email address or Telegram handle, a description of your task and, if you choose, the direction, budget and preferred start.
  • Specialist applications: your name, contact details, specialisation and what you tell us about your experience, including links to a portfolio or CV if you share them.
  • CHYNNO pilot requests, partnership proposals and other messages: your name, contact details, the text of your message and, if you choose, your company name.
  • Technical data processed by our hosting provider when the site is loaded: IP address, browser type, date and time of the request. We do not use this data to identify you.
  • Language preference stored in your browser (see Cookies below).

Providing data is voluntary. A contact and a short description of the task (or of your experience, for specialists) are needed to process your message and reply; all other fields are optional. Without a contact we cannot answer you.

Form submissions are delivered to our mailbox projects@mindforgeukraine.com by the form delivery service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). The website has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

Under the law of Ukraine (Article 11 of the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection"):

  • to reply to your request (including a specialist's application to work with us) and take steps you ask for before a contract, and to perform a contract with you — conclusion and performance of a transaction to which you are a party, or steps preceding it at your request;
  • to communicate with representatives of companies and to keep the website secure — our legitimate interests;
  • once a contract is concluded, to keep accounting and tax records — obligations imposed on us by law.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies:

  • if you contact us about a contract with you personally, including as a specialist — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, or performance of that contract (Art. 6(1)(b));
  • if you write on behalf of a company or visit the website — our legitimate interests in business communication and keeping the site secure (Art. 6(1)(f)). You may object to this processing at any time.

4. Who receives it

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate: our email provider (Google Workspace), the form delivery service FormSubmit, our website host, and — when a project starts — professional advisers bound by confidentiality.

Some of these providers may process data outside Ukraine or the European Economic Area. Before relying on a provider, we check the safeguards it offers for such transfers. You can ask us which providers receive your data and what safeguards apply by writing to projects@mindforgeukraine.com.

5. How long we keep it

Requests and applications that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to 24 months from the last substantive communication. Contract-related data is kept for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes. Where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, certain data may be kept longer, for as long as necessary for that purpose. Our service providers apply their own retention periods to the copies they hold.

6. Your rights

In the cases and within the limits set by applicable law, you may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or, if you are in the EU, with your local data protection authority. Write to projects@mindforgeukraine.com; we respond within the time limits set by applicable law.

7. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site stores your language choice (EN/UA) in your browser so it is remembered on your next visit; you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when serving them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top always shows the current version.
